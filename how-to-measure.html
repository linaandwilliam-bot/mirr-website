THE MEASURING GUIDE

How to measure.

Everything on Mirr rests on two sets of numbers: your body measurements, and the garment's. Both take a couple of minutes to get right — here is exactly how.

FOR SHOPPERS ↓ FOR BRANDS ↓
FOR SHOPPERS

Your four numbers.

The fitting room asks for height, chest, waist, and hips. These four drive every fit score you see.

30 seconds, one tape measure. Measure over underwear or thin clothing, snug but not tight.

Height

No shoes, heels against a wall, standing tall. Measure floor to the top of your head.

COMMON MISTAKE — using the height from an old licence. Measure today; posture changes it.

Chest

Around the fullest part of your chest, tape level all the way round. Breathe normally — measure mid-breath, not puffed out.

COMMON MISTAKE — letting the tape drop at the back. Level means parallel to the floor.

Waist

Your natural waistline — the narrowest point, usually just above the navel. Relax your stomach; do not suck in.

COMMON MISTAKE — measuring where your jeans sit. That is your hip, not your waist.

Hips

Feet together, tape around the fullest part of your seat. Check in a mirror that the tape sits level.

COMMON MISTAKE — measuring at the hip bone. The fullest point is usually lower.

Your measurements stay on your device — the fitting room remembers them so you only do this once.

FOR BRANDS

Flat-lay garment dimensions.

Zip or button the garment fully, spread it flat on a hard surface, and smooth it out — no stretching, no bunching. Measure edge to edge across the flat garment and report those centimetres as they are: we double the flat width for circumference internally, so always submit flat cm, never the doubled figure. Measure your smallest stocked size.

Tops and outerwear — chest armpit to armpit, waist edge to edge, length from the highest shoulder point to the hem.

Bottoms — waist straight across the waistband, hips at the widest point below it, inseam from the crotch seam to the hem.

CHESTStraight across, armpit seam to armpit seam.
WAISTTops: edge to edge at the natural waist of the garment. Bottoms: straight across the top of the waistband, fastened.
HIPSAcross the widest point below the waistband.
LENGTHFrom the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.
INSEAMFrom the crotch seam down the inside of the leg to the hem.
