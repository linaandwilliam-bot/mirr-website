THE PROBLEM

You already know what returns cost you.

We won't quote industry statistics at you — you have your own returns data, and you know how many of those parcels come back over size and fit rather than the product itself. Every one costs you shipping twice, handling, and often a customer who won't order again.

Mirr exists to reduce return rates — not eliminate them; nothing eliminates them — by letting a shopper see your garment on their own body and check the fit against your garment's real measurements before the order is placed, instead of after the parcel arrives.

HOW IT WORKS FOR YOU

Three steps, one of which is real work.

01
Send product photos and garment measurements
Front-facing photos on a plain background, plus flat-lay measurements of each style. We'll be straight with you: measuring garments is real work — a tape measure and a few minutes per style. It's one-time, per style, and it's what makes the fit scores honest. Here's exactly how to measure →
02
We list you
We build your storefront on Mirr — your products, your prices, your branding. No integration work on your side and nothing to install on your own site.
03
Shoppers try on, check their fit, and buy
A shopper uploads one photo of themselves, sees your garment on their own body, and gets a per-measurement fit score with a size recommendation — then buys with a clearer idea of what will arrive.

WHAT WE ARE HONEST ABOUT

The part most pitches leave out.

Mirr is pre-launch. We are onboarding our first brands now. Joining today means a founding rate and a real say in what gets built — not an established audience. We'd rather you know that from this page than find out after you've measured your range.
What the try-on does and doesn't do. The try-on shows your garment on the shopper's body at its design proportions — it's the seeing half. The sizing half is done by the fit scores, which compare your garment's measurements against the shopper's, measurement by measurement.
Fit guidance is guidance. It reduces guesswork; it is not a guarantee. Shoppers see it framed that way too — we don't oversell it to them, and we won't oversell it to you.
